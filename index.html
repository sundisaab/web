Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate?

Any student or early-career developer across India can register — solo or as part of a team.

Can beginners join?

Yes. Mentors are on hand throughout, and problem statements span a range of difficulty levels.

Is it free?

Registration is free, and every participant also receives a month of Wolfram|One at no cost.

Do I need a team?

No. You can register solo and form a team during the Team Formation stage, or bring your own.

How do I access Wolfram|One?

Your activation link and license key are emailed after registration is confirmed.

Will certificates be provided?

Yes — every registered participant receives a verifiable official certificate.
