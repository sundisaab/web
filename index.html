Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our programs

What is the duration of the courses?

Our courses typically range from 3 to 6 months, depending on the program. Each course includes both theoretical learning and hands-on project work to ensure comprehensive understanding.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes, we provide comprehensive placement assistance including resume building, interview preparation, and connecting students with our partner companies. Our dedicated placement cell works closely with students to help them secure positions in top tech companies.

Are the courses suitable for beginners?

Absolutely! Our courses are designed to accommodate students at all skill levels. We start with fundamentals and gradually progress to advanced concepts. Our 1-on-1 mentorship ensures that even complete beginners can successfully learn and apply the concepts.

What is the fee structure for the courses?

Course fees vary depending on the program and duration. We offer flexible payment options and scholarships for deserving students. Please contact our admission counselors for detailed fee structure and available discounts.

Do you provide certificates upon completion?

Yes, we provide industry-recognized certificates upon successful completion of each course. Our certificates are AICTE approved and valued by employers in the tech industry.

What kind of projects will I work on?

You'll work on real-world projects that simulate industry scenarios. These include building complete applications, solving business problems with data analysis, creating machine learning models, and developing full-stack web applications. Our projects are designed to build a strong portfolio that impresses employers.
